FOUND IN THE OLD DRIVER (fixed upstream in 2d6a8ae, "Fixes from a review of
the Switch 2 changes", released as OunceVK 20261118 on 2026-11-18)
  native_chunk_pool_trim (nvkmd_nvgpu_native_chunked.c), run on every free of
  GPU-only memory once the free pool is over 128 MiB: it does a finish flush
  (GPU completes earlier work, dirty L2 lines written back) and then returns
  pool chunks to the process heap. The flush wait releases the owner lock. A
  chunk that another thread frees during that wait sits on top of the pool,
  is not covered by the flush, and is the first one handed back to the heap.
  Its dirty L2 lines can then be written back over heap memory that malloc
  has given to someone else: driver submission records (status 7), batch
  spans (738), our own data. The fix takes the chunks off the pool before the
  flush. Not proven to be our stop (no driver-side trace of the chunk), but
  it is a real corruption path that matches every signature seen since
  turn 35, and stops cluster where memory is freed and re-allocated
  (resizes, map loads, menu close; crash1 creates a new target right then).
