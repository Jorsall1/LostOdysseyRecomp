DIAGNOSIS
  * By handle IDs, the two unregistered threads were created just before
    Guest Main. That matches LO's own unregistered libnx threads from
    Initialize: the exit watchdog (it polls padUpdate / appletMainLoop every
    100 ms), the APM thread and the awake thread.
  * Once resumed after __appExit (hidExit unmaps HID shared memory,
    appletExit closes the applet), the watchdog's next poll touches freed
    memory or a closed service. The process dies before the loader writes
    its report.
  * General rule learned: a thread must not be resumed after the teardown
    unless it is blocked in something the teardown does not wake.
