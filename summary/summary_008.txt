LO_CP_SELF_WAITS (gpu/command_processor.cpp, SelfWaitsEnabled)
  * Default on. A speed shortcut: D3D writes 0xFFFFFEED to marker words with
    one-word MEM_WRITEs and then waits for that same value (WAIT_REG_MEM),
    about 360 times a frame in the busy street. The shortcut treats a write
    plus its own immediate wait as a no-op: it does not advance the "sync
    epoch", which the per-draw caches key on.
  * LO_CP_SELF_WAITS=0 makes every such pair a full sync point. That is
    slower but follows the hardware's ordering more literally.
  * It does NOT decide whether a wait is satisfied. The value is always
    written before the wait reads it.
  * Its value to the user is as a cheap A/B test: it rules the shortcut
    in or out as the cause of the fence-related stall. It is unlikely to fix
    the wheel. I overstated it as the "next step" in turn 7.
