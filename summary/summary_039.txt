CHANGES
  gpu/effect_budget.h (new), wired in gpu/frame_plan.cpp (next to dynamic
  resolution, same gpu_busy total, only with DR on):
    * Level 1 after 2 windows (1 s) above the budget: motion blur off
      (sub_826E1860) + depth of field blur off. Level 2 after another second:
      Dynamic shadows Low (shadow_budget.cpp, 8 largest on-screen casters).
      Back one level after 3 s under 70% of the budget, >= 5 s apart.
      Frames under 3 ms and loads/Settings do not count.
    * Budget = 84% of the frame (14 ms at 60), LO_EFFECT_BUDGET_MS=<ms>;
      LO_EFFECT_BUDGET=0 off. Never turns on what Settings turned off.
    * DoF ordering: renderer tone-map weight goes to 0 first, guest blur
      skip 30 CPU frames later; on restore the blur restarts first and the
      weight returns 30 frames later (no black-blend frame).
    * Log: "effect budget: level N (...; GPU x ms per frame, budget y ms)".
  gpu/frame_plan.cpp: dynamic resolution and the effect budget Hold while
    settings::IsOpen(), as for a load.
  gpu/renderer.cpp (internal size change, no upscaler/FG, shadow multiplier
    unchanged): targets whose scale reference is neither the old scene size,
    its 16:9 area nor the legacy size are kept (shadow maps, 640x640 and other
    fixed surfaces, the 880/440 shadow colour family). Before, every step
    recreated them zero-cleared: a frame of empty shadow map / reset exposure
    per step (a likely cause of shadow flicker on resolution changes). At a
    1280x720 old scene everything is rebuilt as before. LO_DR_KEEP_FIXED=0
    restores the full rebuild. Logged: "scene size step keeps N fixed-size
    targets" (first 8 steps).
