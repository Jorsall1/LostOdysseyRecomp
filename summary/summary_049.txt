FINDING
  thirdparty/plume VulkanFramebuffer took its extent from colour attachment 0
  only. Vulkan requires every attachment to be at least the framebuffer's
  size; a depth smaller than the colour (EDRAM targets grow when a taller
  extent shows up, and colour and depth are sized/grown separately) lets the
  render pass, vkCmdClearAttachments with no rects (ReplayClears / the zero
  clear of a new target) and draws touch memory past the depth image: a GPU
  fault. Whether a smaller depth was actually paired is not proven: the new
  renderer warning will say.
